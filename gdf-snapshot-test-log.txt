==================================================
SUMMARY
==================================================
- GetHistory (Delayed): works correctly for all 4 symbols, including
  LALPATHLAB, at every periodicity tested (DAY, MINUTE). Settles the final
  daily candle some time after close (confirmed next-day for 2026-09-16).
- GetSnapshot (Delayed): works correctly for TCS/RELIANCE/TITAN during
  market hours (~15-19 min observed delay). Decays to all-zero rows for all
  symbols some time after close. Returns all-zero specifically for
  LALPATHLAB during live market hours too, under both symbol and numeric
  token identifiers, while GetHistory confirms LALPATHLAB is actively
  trading at that exact moment - a genuine provider-side gap for this one
  instrument, not reproducible for TCS/RELIANCE/TITAN.
- SubscribeSnapshot (Delayed): subscription accepted, but 0 pushes observed
  in a 65-second window for LALPATHLAB post-close (consistent with the
  GetSnapshot cache being empty for it at that time).
- GetExchangeSnapshot (Delayed): works, single BSE-wide request. Returned
  1627 instruments during market hours, only 97 an hour after close -
  confirms the whole delayed cache shrinks post-close, not just per-symbol
  polling. Diagnostic only, not adopted into production per task scope.
